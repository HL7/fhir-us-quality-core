US Quality Core remains focused on clinical data. This profile is retained in version 1.0.0 as an interim mechanism to support quality measures that use billing-related data elements while longer-term approaches are considered.

The Claim profile **MAY** be used when the relevant billing data is available from an appropriate source system. Its inclusion does not require systems implementing US Quality Core's clinical exchange capabilities to create, obtain, or expose claims data. Claim support is not required by the baseline server capabilities, search APIs, or CapabilityStatement requirements. See [Optional Claim Support](us-quality-core-general-requirements.html#optional-claim-support).
